Meet the Founder

David Bassett

Founder, Covered Bridge Brands

  • 40+ Years in the mortgage industry
  • 1985 Started in the mortgage business
  • 150 Employees at peak

With more than 40 years in the mortgage industry, David Bassett has spent his career building businesses, solving problems, and understanding what it takes to make a business succeed. Since starting in the mortgage business in 1985, he has built and operated companies with as many as 150 employees and has continued to invest in and operate businesses across multiple industries. His business interests today include commercial properties and a flooring company that has been serving its market for more than 50 years.

Covered Bridge Brands grew out of David’s own experience as a business owner. Like many business owners, he wanted better marketing and better results—but became frustrated with complicated systems, unnecessary services, high fees, and marketing companies that seemed more interested in selling products than solving problems.

David built CBB around a different philosophy: business owners should know exactly what they are getting, why they need it, and how it is helping their business. CBB develops practical AI-powered marketing and lead-generation systems designed to produce real business results without unnecessary technology, confusing jargon, or bloated costs.

His goal is simple: deliver significantly more value than the client pays for. Because, in David’s view, if a business owner cannot clearly see the value, they won't remain a client for long.

Outside of Business

Outside of business, David's faith and family are central to his life. He and his wife have two daughters, two sons-in-law, and one grandchild.
