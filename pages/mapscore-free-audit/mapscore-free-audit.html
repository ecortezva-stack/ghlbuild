What You'll Learn

A Clearer View of Your Local Visibility

When people nearby search for what you offer, your Google Business Profile is often the first thing they see. The audit gives you a plain-language look at where you stand.

  • The searches that matter to you

    See how your business profile shows up for the keywords you choose—the services and phrases your customers actually search for.

  • The area you actually serve

    Focus the audit on your location and a radius that fits your market, so the results reflect the customers you want to reach.

  • A starting point for next steps

    Review your results on your own or with a CBB marketing analyst, then decide what, if anything, is worth working on next.

Any search-volume or dollar figures shown in your results are estimates. They don't reflect your actual customer spending and aren't a guarantee of rankings or revenue.
