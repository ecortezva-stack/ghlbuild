Questions

Frequently asked questions

Have a question that isn't covered here? Call or text 814-893-1075 or email david@coveredbridgebrands.com.

What does MapScore include?

Google Business Profile optimization and posting, review tools and responses, social and video publishing to supported accounts, AI search visibility reporting, directory citations and listing sync, SEO recommendations and widgets for your existing website, ranking heatmaps, keyword tracking and competitor analysis, plus account-manager updates and CBB support.

How does the free guided audit work?

You book a 30-minute call. At the scheduled time, your CBB marketing analyst shares the audit, helps you complete it, explains the results and introduces MapScore. Booking the call doesn't run an audit by itself; you complete it together during the call.

Does the audit sign me up for MapScore?

No. The audit is free and doesn't start a subscription or give us access to your Google account. You decide afterward whether you'd like to go ahead.

How is MapScore priced if I have more than one location?

MapScore is $395 per month for each business location. Each additional location needs its own plan.

Is there a setup fee or a contract?

There's no setup fee and no term commitment; MapScore is a monthly service. Payments already made are non-refundable.

Will MapScore work with my current website?

Yes. We provide SEO recommendations for the website you already have, plus supported review, photo and post widgets you can add to it.

What account access will you need?

After you subscribe, you'll connect your Google Business Profile and any supported social or video accounts you'd like included, such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube or TikTok. Features that publish to an account start once that account is connected.

Can I approve content before it goes live?

Yes. During onboarding you set your approval preferences for content and review replies, along with your tone and priorities. You'll receive approval requests according to those settings.

How will I know what's being done?

You'll get account-manager updates, reminders and reports, including ranking heatmaps, keyword tracking and action logs. The CBB team is also available for guidance and questions.

Can you remove negative reviews?

We can help you respond professionally and flag reviews that may break Google's policies. Google decides whether a flagged review is removed, so removal isn't guaranteed.

Who is MapScore for?

Local businesses across the United States that want to be found by people searching nearby. The guided audit is a good way to see whether MapScore fits your business.
