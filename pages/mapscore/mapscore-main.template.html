The Challenge

Local marketing needs regular attention

When someone nearby searches for what you offer, your Google Business Profile, reviews and listings often shape their first impression. Hours change, services evolve, new reviews arrive and competitors stay active. Information that was right last year can quietly fall out of date.

A one-time setup is only the beginning.

MapScore brings that ongoing work into one managed service from Covered Bridge Brands, coordinated around your preferences and the accounts you connect.

  1. 01

    Accurate business details

    Keep your information current as your business changes.

  2. 02

    Fresh posts, photos and video

    Give people useful, relevant information about your services and offers.

  3. 03

    Reviews and responses

    Ask for feedback and respond consistently, in line with your preferences.

  4. 04

    Visibility you can see

    Track where you appear for your keywords and adjust the strategy as things change.

What's Included

Every service. One plan.

MapScore includes every service in the Paige agency platform except the website builder, plus guidance and support from CBB. Here's what that covers.

  • Google Business Profile

    • Profile optimization, including business descriptions, services, attributes and social links
    • Scheduled Google posts and offers
    • Photos and videos published to your profile
    • Content written for your business, services and location

  • Reviews and reputation

    • Review request tools and reminders to help you ask happy customers for feedback
    • AI-assisted review responses that follow your approval settings
    • New-review alerts
    • Flagging reviews that may break Google's policies (Google decides whether to remove them)
    • Supported reputation features, such as sharing positive reviews as social posts

  • Social media and video

    • Social publishing to supported Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn accounts
    • Video creation for your business
    • Publishing to supported YouTube and TikTok accounts

  • Directory listings and citations

    • Citation creation on business directories
    • Directory listings kept in sync with your business details
    • Business updates shared across supported listings

  • AI search visibility

    • Visibility checks and reporting across supported AI platforms, such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity
    • Clearer, consistent business information for AI assistants to draw on

  • Your existing website

    • SEO recommendations for the website you already have
    • Supported review, photo and post widgets you can add to your site

  • Rankings, reporting and strategy

    • Google Maps ranking heatmaps and keyword tracking
    • Before-and-after comparisons, reports and action logs
    • Competitor analysis and strategy adjustments

  • Updates, approvals and CBB support

    • AI account-manager updates, reminders, approval requests and reports
    • Approval preferences you set for content and review replies
    • Guidance and support from the Covered Bridge Brands team
    • Approved CBB extras, such as the CBB-branded newsletter, at no additional charge

Not included: the website builder. MapScore works with the website you already have.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Have a question that isn't covered here? Contact us or call or text 814-893-1075.

What does MapScore include?

Every Paige service except the website builder: Google Business Profile optimization and posting, review tools and responses, social and video publishing to supported accounts, AI search visibility reporting, directory citations and listing sync, SEO recommendations and widgets for your existing website, ranking heatmaps, keyword tracking and competitor analysis, plus account-manager updates and CBB support.

How is MapScore priced if I have more than one location?

MapScore is $395 per month for each business location. Each additional location needs its own plan.

Is there a setup fee or a contract?

There's no setup fee and no term commitment; MapScore is a monthly service. Payments already made are non-refundable.

Do you build websites?

The website builder isn't part of MapScore. We work with the website you already have, providing SEO recommendations and supported review, photo and post widgets you can add to it.

What account access will you need?

After you subscribe, you'll connect your Google Business Profile and any supported social or video accounts you'd like included, such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube or TikTok. Features that publish to an account can't run until that account is connected. Running the free audit doesn't give us access to any account.

Can I approve content before it goes live?

Yes. During onboarding you set your approval preferences for content and review replies, along with your tone and priorities. You'll receive approval requests according to those settings.

How will I know what's being done?

You'll get account-manager updates, reminders and reports, including ranking heatmaps, keyword tracking and action logs. The CBB team is also available for guidance and questions.

What's the difference between the guided call and the free audit?

Both use the same free audit. On the guided call, a CBB officer walks you through it and discusses the findings with you. The free audit on your own lets you run it any time from our Free Audit page, with no booking needed.

What happens on the guided audit call?

The call takes about 30 minutes. At the scheduled time, your CBB sales officer shares our Free Audit page, you complete the audit while you talk, and then you discuss the findings and possible next steps. Booking the call doesn't run an audit by itself.

Does completing the audit sign me up for MapScore?

No. The audit is free and doesn't start a subscription or give us access to your Google account. You decide afterward whether you'd like to go ahead.

Can you remove negative reviews?

We can help you respond professionally and flag reviews that may break Google's policies. Google decides whether a flagged review is removed, so removal isn't guaranteed.

What does the six-month guarantee mean?

If you don't see measurable improvement in your local search rankings within 6 months, we'll keep working with you until you do, at no additional cost. It's a continued-service guarantee, not a refund, and it doesn't promise a specific ranking or sales.

Who is MapScore for?

Local businesses across the United States that want to be found by people searching nearby. The free audit is a good way to see whether MapScore fits your business.
