## Telephone Calls, Text Messages, and Consent
When you submit an inquiry or contact us, we may respond by email or, if you provide a telephone number, by telephone regarding your inquiry, requested services, scheduling, or related follow-up, subject to applicable law.
Providing a telephone number or submitting an inquiry does not, by itself, give consent to receive text messages from us.
If you explicitly opt in to text messages from COVERED BRIDGE BRANDS LLC, you may receive promotional offers, discounts, and service updates through our text-message marketing program, as described in our Terms of Service.
Consent to receive text messages is optional and is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.
Text messages may be sent using text-messaging and CRM software. We do not use automated, AI, or prerecorded voice calls.
Message and data rates may apply to text messages. Message frequency may vary.
You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may reply HELP for assistance with text messages.
You may request that we stop contacting you by telephone by asking us not to call you or by contacting us using the information provided below.
Opting out of text messages or telephone calls does not necessarily prevent us from communicating with you through another method you have provided, where permitted by law and where reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry or maintain an existing business relationship.
No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
Where applicable, Covered Bridge Brands maintains records of consent and opt-out requests for compliance and business-record purposes.
